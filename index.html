Cloud Save+ Account Deletion

Cloud Save+ users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to cloudsave.ng@gmail.com using the email address connected to your Cloud Save+ account.

Use the subject: Cloud Save+ Account Deletion Request

We may contact you to verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

Data deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, your Cloud Save+ account and associated cloud-stored user data will be deleted, except information that we may be required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes.
